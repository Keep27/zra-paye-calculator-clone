# ZRA PAYE Calculator

A web-based PAYE tax calculator that replicates the Zambia Revenue Authority calculator. Calculates salary deductions including NAPSA, NHIMA, and PAYE tax.

## About This Project

This is a web development assignment that clones the official ZRA PAYE calculator website. It calculates employee tax deductions based on 2026 Zambian tax rates.

## How It Works

The calculator takes your salary information and calculates:
- **Gross Pay** - Basic salary plus allowances
- **NAPSA** - 5% pension contribution
- **NHIMA** - 1% health insurance
- **PAYE Tax** - Progressive tax based on income bands
- **Net Salary** - Your take-home pay

## Tax Rates (2026)

| Income Range | Tax Rate |
|--------------|----------|
| K0 - K5,100 | 0% |
| K5,100.01 - K7,100 | 20% |
| K7,100.01 - K9,200 | 30% |
| K9,200.01+ | 37% |

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Enter your **Basic Pay**
3. Enter **Allowances** (optional)
4. Enter **Statutory Contribution** (optional)
5. View your results automatically
6. Click **Clear** to reset

## Example

**Input:**
- Basic Pay: K10,000
- Allowances: K2,000

**Output:**
- Gross Pay: K12,000
- NAPSA: K600
- NHIMA: K120
- PAYE Tax: K1,800
- **Net Salary: K9,480**
